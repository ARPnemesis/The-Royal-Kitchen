# Turkey Shepherd's Pie with Cheddar-Chive Mash

*British comfort · weekend · ~70 min (~40 min active) · ~45g protein · ~580 cal per serving · **4 servings (batch)***

**Rated 5★.** Oven-baked casseroles are the best-reheating format on record, and mashed potatoes are the roof.

## Ingredients (4 servings — one 9×13 pan)

**Filling**
- 2½ lb 93/7 ground turkey — 🧊 **FREEZE ON ARRIVAL.** Ground poultry is the most perishable item on the list and this dish cooks late in the week (Sat). Freeze the day you shop; move to the fridge Friday night. *Sanctioned fallback: 85/15 ground beef (makes it a cottage pie).*
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 yellow onions, diced
- 4 carrots, diced small
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 tbsp tomato paste
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 3 cups chicken stock
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce — *fallback: 4 tsp soy sauce*
- 2 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried rosemary, crushed
- 2 cups frozen peas
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**Cheddar-chive mash**
- 4 lb russet potatoes, peeled, 2-inch chunks
- 8 tbsp (1 stick) butter
- ⅔ cup whole milk, warmed
- 1½ cups (6 oz) shredded sharp cheddar
- 2 tbsp dried chives — *fallback: 4 green onions, green parts*
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

## Method
1. Boil potatoes in salted water 15–18 min until fork-tender; drain, steam dry 2 min.
2. Meanwhile brown turkey in oil in a large oven-safe pot, 8 min. Add onion and carrot, 5 min; garlic, thyme, rosemary, 1 min.
3. Stir in paste 1 min; sprinkle flour, stir 1 min. Add stock and Worcestershire; simmer 6–8 min until gravy coats a spoon. Stir in peas, salt, pepper.
4. Mash potatoes with butter and warm milk; fold in cheddar, chives, season.
5. Transfer filling to a 9×13 dish, spread mash over it, ridge with a fork.
6. Bake 400°F, 25 min until bubbling and golden; broil 2 min. **Rest 10 min.**

## Notes
- Vegetable ceiling: carrots and peas as listed — nothing more.
- Cut into 4 portions; portion 4 is frozen on cook day.

## Reheat Notes
- **Reheat:** 350°F oven, **uncovered**, 15–18 min — re-crisps the potato roof. Microwave covered 2½ min in a pinch (loses the roof).
- **Holds:** 4 days refrigerated.
- **Freezes and thaws well** (mash-topped pies freeze fine). Portion 4 → freezer on cook day. Thaw overnight in the fridge.
- **Yield in meals:** 4 dinners (the 2-serving version made 3 meals — this batch may stretch further).
