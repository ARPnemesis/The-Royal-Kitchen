# Filipino Chicken Inasal Bowls
*Filipino · Weeknight · 30 min (15 min marinate) · ~42g protein · ~470 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1.25 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, pounded to even thickness
- 3 tbsp calamansi juice (or lime juice — sanctioned fallback)
- 2 tbsp white vinegar
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp annatto/achiote oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1.5 cups jasmine rice
- 4 cloves garlic, minced (for the rice)
- 2 tbsp neutral oil (for the rice)
- 2 scallions, sliced, for garnish
- Vinegar dipping sauce: 3 tbsp white vinegar, 1 clove garlic minced, 1/2 tsp sugar, pinch chili flakes

## Method
1. Whisk calamansi juice, vinegar, garlic, ginger, brown sugar, soy sauce, half the achiote oil, salt, and pepper. Marinate chicken 15 min at room temp (or up to overnight if prepping ahead).
2. Cook the rice. While it cooks, heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in a skillet and fry the minced garlic until golden and fragrant; set the crispy bits aside for topping, then fry the cooked rice 2–3 min in the same garlicky oil.
3. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high. Sear the chicken 5–6 min per side, basting with the remaining achiote oil, until charred at the edges and cooked through (165°F internal).
4. Rest the chicken 5 min, slice. Serve over the garlic fried rice, topped with the fried garlic bits and scallions, with the vinegar dipping sauce alongside.

## Notes
- **Reheat: 350°F oven, covered, 12 min**, or skillet over medium 3–4 min to re-crisp the edges — avoid the microwave, it steams away the char.
- Yield: ~2 meals (1 dinner + 1 lunch).
- **Identity ingredient: achiote/annatto oil** — this is what makes it Inasal rather than just grilled chicken. Sanctioned fallback if unavailable: 2 tbsp neutral oil + 1 tsp paprika + pinch of turmeric for color.
- Zero Greek yogurt.
- First Filipino dish since Chicken Adobo (5★) — the Critic flagged Filipino as a cuisine worth expanding.
