# Cuban Ropa Vieja with Rice & Black Beans
*Cuban · Weekend · ~2 hr (mostly hands-off) · ~42g protein · ~545 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (4 servings)
- 2 lb flank steak (or skirt steak), trimmed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, sliced thin
- 2 bell peppers (1 red, 1 green), sliced — **season with salt and pepper before the pre-cook** (standing request)
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ cup green olives, sliced (pimento-stuffed is fine)
- 1 tbsp capers, drained (optional)
- 2 tbsp bottled lime juice
- 2 cups white rice
- 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, to finish
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Season the beef generously with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high; sear the steak 3–4 min per side until browned. Remove.
2. Add onion and the seasoned bell peppers to the pot; cook 5 min until softened. Add garlic, 1 min more.
3. Return the steak. Add crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, beef broth, bay leaf, cumin, oregano, and smoked paprika. Bring to a simmer, cover, reduce heat to low, and braise 1½–2 hr until the steak shreds easily with a fork.
4. Remove the steak, shred with two forks, and return to the pot. Stir in olives, capers, and lime juice. Simmer uncovered 5–10 min to thicken slightly.
5. Meanwhile, cook the rice (2 cups rice to 3½ cups water, standard method) and warm the black beans.
6. Serve the shredded beef and sauce over rice with black beans alongside. Finish with cilantro.

## Reheat Notes
Reheat: 350°F oven, covered, 20 min, or microwave, covered, 2–3 min with a splash of broth or water. Holds 3+ days refrigerated — the shredded beef and sauce only improve. **Freezes well** — portion 4 goes to the freezer same day (Rule 5), not the fridge; thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.

## Notes
- Sanctioned fallback: no fresh bell peppers → frozen pepper strips, thawed and drained.
- Vegetable ceiling: 2 peppers total across a 4-serving batch, within range.
