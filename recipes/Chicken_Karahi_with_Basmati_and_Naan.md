# Chicken Karahi with Basmati & Naan

*Indian / Pakistani · weeknight · ~40 min active · ~50g protein · ~575 cal per serving — scaled to 4 servings (batch-cook model)*

> 🍛 Indian is a 5★ cuisine in this kitchen on the strength of butter chicken twice — but butter chicken's one flaw was the yogurt separating on reheat. Karahi is the opposite build: **tomato, ginger and green chili, cooked down hard in ghee, with no yogurt and no cream in the sauce body at all.** It reheats better than butter chicken and hits harder — which is exactly why it's the batch-cook pick this week over Weeknight Butter Chicken.

## Ingredients (4 servings)

**Chicken & masala**
- 2½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into 1½-inch chunks
- 6 tbsp ghee — *sanctioned fallback: butter, or 4 tbsp neutral oil*
- 2 yellow onions, sliced thin
- 2 cans (14 oz each) diced tomatoes, drained — *using the canned fallback at this batch size for reliability; 12 roma tomatoes, chopped, work too — add 12–15 min to step 4's cook-down*
- 2 green bell peppers, cut in wide strips — **salt and pepper them as they go into the pan, not after**
- 4 serrano chilies, split lengthwise — *fallback: 2 jalapeños; leave the seeds in if you want the heat*
- 4 tbsp ginger, minced, plus more julienned to finish
- 12 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chili powder or cayenne
- 2 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed between your palms — *if the store doesn't have it, **leave it out rather than substituting**; nothing else tastes like it and the dish is still very good without it*
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**To serve**
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 8 pieces naan
- 1 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 lemons, in wedges

## Method

1. Start the basmati: rinse until the water runs clear, then cook 2 cups rice to 3½ cups water — boil, cover, lowest heat 15 minutes, rest 5 off the heat.
2. Heat the ghee in a wide heavy skillet or wok over medium-high. **Sear the chicken in two batches** so it doesn't crowd the pan and steam — 4–5 minutes per batch, turning once, until it takes color. Pull it out — it will finish in the sauce.
3. Add the onions and the **seasoned** bell peppers; cook 5–6 minutes until softened and browning at the edges. Add the minced ginger, garlic, and serranos; 1 minute more.
4. Add the tomatoes and 2 tsp salt. Turn the heat up and cook **10–12 minutes**, mashing the tomatoes against the pan, until they collapse into a thick masala and the ghee visibly separates and pools at the edges. **This step is the whole dish — do not rush it.** If it looks like watery tomato sauce, keep going.
5. Stir in the coriander, cumin, turmeric, chili powder, and black pepper; 1 minute.
6. Return the chicken and any resting juices. Simmer 8–10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through (**165°F**) and the sauce clings rather than pools.
7. Off the heat, stir in the garam masala and crushed kasuri methi. Rest 3 minutes.
8. Top with julienned ginger and cilantro. Serve over basmati with warm naan and lemon wedges.

## Reheat Notes
**Reheat: microwave, covered, 2–3 minutes**, with a splash of water stirred into the sauce first, or a covered skillet over medium-low 5–6 min. No dairy in the sauce means nothing to split — this holds up better over 3+ days than butter chicken ever did. Warm the naan 60 seconds in a dry pan; do not microwave it. **Freezes well** (no dairy to break) — portion 4 goes to the freezer same day (Rule 5), not the fridge; thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.

## Notes
- **Zero yogurt in this dish**, sauce or marinade. Deliberate.
- **Vegetable ceiling:** 2 onions, 2 peppers across a 4-serving batch — proportional to the original, still under the ceiling.
- Perishability: raw chicken breast holds about four days. This week it's scheduled mid-week (Wednesday), so no freeze-on-arrival needed; in a future week landing Thursday or later, **freeze it on arrival and thaw in the fridge the night before** — it slices cleaner half-frozen anyway.
- Breast is used here on purpose to vary the cut, but boneless thighs work and are more forgiving; add 3 minutes to step 6.
- Yield: 4 servings. Portion 4 → freezer (Rule 5).
