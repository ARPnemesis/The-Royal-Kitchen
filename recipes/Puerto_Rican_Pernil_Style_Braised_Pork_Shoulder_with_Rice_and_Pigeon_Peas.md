# Puerto Rican Pernil-Style Braised Pork Shoulder with Rice & Pigeon Peas
*Puerto Rican · Weekend · 2 hr (mostly hands-off) · ~46g protein · ~580 cal per serving — scaled to 4 servings (batch-cook model)*

## Ingredients (4 servings)
- 2½ lb boneless pork shoulder, cut into 2-inch chunks
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp adobo seasoning
- ¼ cup sofrito (store-bought or homemade)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp achiote (annatto) oil
- 1–2 bay leaves
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 2 cans (15 oz each) pigeon peas (gandules), drained
- 2 cups long-grain rice
- 1 green bell pepper, diced — **season with salt and pepper before the pre-cook** (standing request)
- ½ cup pimento-stuffed olives, chopped (optional)
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Season the diced bell pepper with salt and pepper before it goes in (standing request). Toss pork chunks with adobo seasoning, minced garlic, and half the sofrito; let sit 15 min if time allows.
2. Heat olive oil and achiote oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high. Brown pork in batches (don't crowd the pot), ~8–10 min total. Remove.
3. Add bell pepper and remaining sofrito to the pot, sauté 3–4 min. Return pork, add bay leaf(ves) and broth. Cover, reduce heat to low, braise 1½–2 hr until fork-tender.
4. Stir in rice, pigeon peas, and olives. Add water if needed to just cover the rice. Cover and simmer 22–25 min until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed.
5. Rest 5 min, fluff, discard bay leaf(ves), serve.

## Reheat Notes
Reheat: 350°F oven, covered, 20–25 min, or microwave, covered, 3 min (add a splash of water or broth to loosen the rice). Holds 3+ days refrigerated — the pork and beans only improve; the rice softens slightly but holds acceptably. **Freezes well** — portion 4 goes to the freezer same day (Rule 5), not the fridge; thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating, adding a splash of broth to revive the rice.
