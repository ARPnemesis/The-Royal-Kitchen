RULE 1 â€” REHEAT HOLD (hard constraint; reject a candidate regardless of how well it scores on taste or macros if it fails this)
Every dinner dish must hold **3+ days refrigerated and reheat without texture collapse.**
- **Disqualified by default:** fried or crispy-skin items, delicate fish (salmon, white fish), dishes plated on fresh greens, anything where the texture depends on being served within minutes of cooking.
- **Qualified by default:** braises, stews, chilis, curries, casseroles, shredded/pulled proteins, bean and lentil dishes, meat sauces, most slow-cooker output.
- Component dishes (bowls) qualify **only if** the recipe doc specifies which components are stored separately and assembled at eat time (e.g. protein and sauce refrigerated together, rice reheated separately, fresh garnish added at serving).

RULE 2 â€” ACTIVE-TIME BIAS

RULE 3 â€” NO ORPHAN PERISHABLES (the waste fix)
